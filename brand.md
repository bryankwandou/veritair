## Typography — Geist + Geist Mono

- **Display + body:** Geist
- **Mono (numbers, addresses, tx signatures):** Geist Mono

Already wired by `create-next-app` via `next/font/google` in `app/layout.tsx` (variables `--font-geist-sans`, `--font-geist-mono`). Chosen deliberately over swapping to another pair: Geist is Vercel's own typeface, and Vercel is one of the calibration references named in the project plan for this exact "Silicon Valley clean" register.

## Tone and voice

### Words to use

Direct, specific, number-forward, factual. Prefer verbs over adjectives, nouns over metaphors. Say what a thing does, not how it feels. Measured and patient — never urgent unless the situation genuinely is (a locked order, an expired escrow). Sparse: one sentence per idea, lists over paragraphs, numbers over adjectives. Restrained even when the news is good — "confirmed" carries more weight than "amazing."

### Words to avoid

Hype words (revolutionary, game-changing, unleash, supercharge, seamless), "unlock" as a verb, "leverage," "empower," "in today's fast-paced world," exclamation marks, emoji, manufactured urgency ("act now," "limited time"), stacked adjectives, and any AI-tell filler that describes effort instead of outcome ("simply," "just," "easily").

### Voice example

> Order confirmed. 0.025 SOL escrowed. Ridgeline Hub will assign a drone once your pad opens for arrivals.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**

- Use the shadcn CSS variables (`bg-background`, `text-foreground`, `bg-primary`) everywhere. Never hardcode hex.
- Keep the 4px spacing grid (`gap-2`, `p-4`, `px-6`).
- Respect `prefers-color-scheme` via the `.dark` class on `<html>`.
- Use `font-mono tabular-nums` for weights, prices, and transaction signatures.
- Test every component in both light and dark mode before considering it done.

**Don't:**

- Hardcode colors (`#4FB8D4`, `rgb(...)`) in component files.
- Use `transition: all` — always specify `transition-colors`, `transition-transform`, etc.
- Add a gradient anywhere — this palette's mood doesn't call for one.
- Override shadcn tokens per-component — fix `globals.css` once instead.
- Reach for exclamation marks or emoji in any shipped copy.
